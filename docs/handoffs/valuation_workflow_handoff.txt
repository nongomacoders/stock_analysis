PURPOSE
This application separates research extraction, analyst-approved forecast assumptions, deterministic valuation, target approval, and publication. Gemini may extract evidence and suggest assumptions, but it must not be the authoritative calculator for deterministic valuations or silently carry prior report assumptions forward.

CORE GOVERNANCE
- Deep Research reports are versioned and append-only.
- Previous Deep Research reports are not supplied to Gemini during a rerun.
- A previous report is archived only after a successful replacement.
- ForecastPlan assumptions have explicit origin and approval state.
- Proposed assumptions are not valuation inputs.
- Analyst assumptions require explicit acceptance.
- Gemini suggestions require explicit acceptance or rejection.
- Approved ForecastPlan versions are immutable.
- Corrections happen by cloning to a new draft version.
- Valuation execution requires an approved, persisted ForecastPlan and deterministic preflight.
- Deterministic valuation results are append-only and keyed by plan, engine version, and input hash.
- Deterministic valuations do not overwrite legacy Gemini targets.
- Target approval and publication are separate explicit actions.
- No workflow action should approve a plan, run a valuation, create a target, or publish automatically unless the user explicitly invokes that action.

Semantics:
- trading_margin = trading_profit / sale_of_merchandise
- forecast sale of merchandise =
  historical sale of merchandise * (1 + accepted sale_of_merchandise_growth)
- forecast trading profit =
  forecast sale of merchandise * accepted trading_margin

Do not substitute:
- retail_sales_growth for sale_of_merchandise_growth
- revenue_growth for sale_of_merchandise_growth
- operating_margin for trading_margin

TRU FY2026 historical baseline:
- sale of merchandise: R21.339bn
- trading profit: R2.774bn
- disclosed trading margin: 13.0%
- R21.339bn * 13.0% is approximately R2.774bn, allowing disclosure rounding

The reported 19.2% operating margin must not be applied to R23.030bn accounting revenue as the primary deterministic retail DCF margin.

Finance income is excluded from trading profit:
- interest income and dividend income remain non-trading items

Direct EBIT/FCFF route:
- sale_of_merchandise_growth + trading_margin
  -> forecast_trading_profit
  -> direct EBIT
- depreciation -> D&A add-back
- tax_rate -> cash tax
- total_capex -> capex
- working_capital -> change in operating working capital
- other_recurring_cash -> recurring operating cash deduction
- forecast_revenue can remain informational/cross-check data on the direct trading-profit route

17. IMPORTANT KNOWN FAILURE MODES

- Calling a previous Gemini report “Baseline Truth.”
- Sending a previous report into a rerun prompt.
- Mixing FY2025 AFS with FY2026 SENS.
- Using first filename/directory order for source selection.
- Treating management target as formal guidance.
- Applying operating margin to the wrong revenue denominator.
- Treating retail_sales_growth, revenue_growth, and sale_of_merchandise_growth as interchangeable.
- Aggregating Group plus segments and double counting.
- Omitting a known material segment or FX conversion.
- Using weighted-average shares when current issued shares are required, or vice versa.
- Applying current issued shares without a dilution warning.
- Treating finance income as trading profit.
- Treating leases inconsistently between FCFF and equity bridge.
- Supplying both net_cash and net_debt.
- Adding ordinary receivables/inventory as non-operating assets.
- Marking READY when the execution-normalized plan has no periods or terminal method.
- Persisting zero-value NOT_CALCULABLE placeholders before preflight.
- Persisting ephemeral derived metric IDs that cannot resolve after reload.
- Editing approved versions instead of cloning.
- Accepting unused proposals merely to pass approval.
- Auto-running valuation after save/approve.
- Publishing deterministic targets into report text without explicit approval.
