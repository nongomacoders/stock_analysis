PURPOSE
The Historical Backtest system reconstructs the evidence and market data available at a historical as-of date, isolates that work from live ForecastPlans and valuations, and supports separate historical plans/results. It must never query yfinance during a backtest rerun and must not leak current reports, current ForecastPlans, learning points, later evidence, or later market prices.
