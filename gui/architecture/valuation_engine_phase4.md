# Phase 4 deterministic valuation engine v1.0

New valuations live in the append-only `deterministic_valuations` table. New report publication and its deterministic valuation insert occur in one transaction. They reference a deep-research version but never update `stock_analysis.deepresearch`, its date/pointer, or old Gemini targets. Every run stores engine version, timestamp, source report, candidate/input IDs, explicit plan, preflight, method schedules, reconciliation and output. `published_at` is populated only for PASS/PASS_WITH_WARNINGS results after `recalculate_target` succeeds. FAIL/NOT_CALCULABLE rows remain audit records with no deterministic target.

The engine entry point resolves `InputRef` objects to Phase 3 `ValuationInputCandidate` records and reruns Python eligibility. It accepts only eligible or eligible-with-warning values from the same report version/case. Company disclosures additionally need the Phase 1 verified source excerpt/ID; model assumptions need both provenance and rationale or a permitted recorded override. The retained comparison metric set makes freshness decisions reproducible. Missing references yield method-level NOT_CALCULABLE. Ineligible or BLOCKING inputs yield FAIL. An explicit `ValuationPlan` provides bear/base/bull case schedules and chooses one primary method; DCF and SOTP are not averaged. Scenario differences retain metric IDs, selected values, units, rationale, provenance and eligibility. Optional cross-check methods do not silently substitute for a missing primary method.

A mining forecast can bridge ROM ? grade ? contained metal ? recovery ? recovered metal ? processing conversion ? saleable metal. Direct contained or saleable company guidance can enter at the appropriate stage. Development capacity is prorated from the start date with explicit utilization, ramp-up and ownership. Commodity revenue uses saleable tonnes ? benchmark USD/t ? realization ? payability less treatment charge, then explicit FX where required. Current spot cannot silently supply a long-term forecast. Operating costs are separately identified (mining, processing, refining, transport, treatment, royalties, other operating) with corporate cost and sustaining capex apart. Reported AISC is a separately defined cross-check, not a renamed production-cost metric.

Accounting earnings are revenue less operating and corporate costs, depreciation, finance and tax, with ownership/minorities where supplied. EPS requires weighted-average shares; HEPS additionally requires headline adjustments, else HEPS_NOT_CALCULABLE. Unlevered FCF is EBIT less cash tax plus depreciation, less sustaining capex, growth capex, working-capital increase and other recurring cash items. WACC is equity weight ? (risk-free + beta ? equity premium + country premium) plus debt weight ? cost of debt ? (1-tax). Currency, nominal/real and inflation bases must agree with cash flow. USD enterprise value needs an explicit eligible FX rate/pair before ZAR equity reconciliation; per-tonne prices cannot be used as total cash costs. An eligible supported WACC assumption can substitute for missing components, but the unsupported Jubilee 12% cannot.

DCF discounts each explicit forecast FCF and exactly one terminal value: next-period FCF/(WACC-g) with g<WACC, or a stated terminal EBIT/EBITDA ? exit multiple. SOTP values unique asset boundaries as gross value ? ownership ? explicit probability ? discount factor. Deferred proceeds are dated payment amounts ? completion probability ? counterparty factor / (1+rate)^years. Alternative settlement scenarios remain separate. The engine checks duplicate asset boundaries, disposal proceeds alongside continued asset value, group-DCF overlap, and cash/debt/receivables also present in the equity bridge. Probability-weighted project values cannot also use a declared risk-loaded discount rate.

Enterprise/operating value plus non-operating assets, receivables and cash, less debt, leases and minorities, plus other equity adjustments gives equity value. Forward diluted shares are preferred; current issued shares produce a dilution warning; historical weighted-average shares are rejected. Equity/shares gives unrounded ZAR per share, rounded ZAR and cents. `recalculate_target` checks every term and the stored target within ZAR0.005. The result object alone renders valuation numbers. A post-Python Gemini prompt is read-only; Gemini cannot alter that object or inject a new target. When a current share price is supplied, Python feeds the reconciled target back into the Phase 3 >100%/>200% upside review. The pre-Python research prompt strips old target/HEPS requests and rejects numeric Gemini target lines.

`run_deterministic_valuation.py` accepts explicit source-linked JSON plans and inputs. The first Jubilee inspection has no defensible complete plan: Roan guidance and newer issued shares pass preflight, but saleable recovery/realization, complete FCF/WACC, current equity adjustments and component values are unavailable. The separate result is NOT_CALCULABLE and retains ZAR1.70 as a historical Gemini target only. The component availability schedule and reasons are in `tmp/jubilee_phase4_valuation.json`. `tmp/phase4_synthetic_sensitivity.json` demonstrates recalculated WACC/growth and operating-factor sensitivities; it is not Jubilee data.

Known v1.0 limits: no loss carryforward, detailed tax jurisdiction or debt schedule; no automated source-document ingestion for all component assumptions; no extraction of a validated Project G or Molefe mine plan; no automatic allocation of central costs across overlapping assets. Margin/valuation multiple checks need relevant supplied comparators. FX sensitivity requires explicit revenue and cost currency exposures. A future Phase 5 should add analyst-reviewed input-plan authoring, source quote binding for every forecast assumption, component-level forecast calendars, tax/financing detail, and a GUI comparison of legacy versus gated deterministic targets. Engine versions must be immutable once published; calculation changes require a new version.
