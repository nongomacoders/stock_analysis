================================================================
1. WHAT THIS PROJECT DOES
================================================================
Desktop "JSE Command Center" for Johannesburg Stock Exchange analysis:

- Watchlist monitoring of JSE tickers (prices, research state, action-log alerts)
- Price charting + technical analysis (matplotlib windows)
- Fundamental research: SENS announcements, strategy/research notes,
  versioned append-only Deep Research reports, results PDFs
- Deterministic valuation workflow with analyst-approved ForecastPlans
  (LLM suggests, deterministic engine calculates - no auto-approve)
- Historical backtesting isolated from live plans/valuations
- Portfolio tracking / discipline (holdings, transactions, metrics)
- Background market monitoring + ingestion (prices, SENS, news,
  results, commodities/FX) into PostgreSQL
